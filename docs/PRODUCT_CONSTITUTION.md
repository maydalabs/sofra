# Product constitution

## Promise

> Be welcomed into a Turkish household and join the table.

Sofra's inventory is a scheduled table: a household, verified participating host, specific date and time, host-selected menu, approved capacity, format, atmosphere, practical context, and availability. It is not a restaurant listing, cooking class, tour meal, or staged performance.

## Non-negotiable experience rules

1. The household chooses the complete menu. Travelers disclose dietary needs privately so the existing menu can be assessed for compatibility; they do not order or customize dishes.
2. At least one verified adult host meaningfully joins the dinner or the tea-and-conversation period.
3. The expected rhythm is welcome, introductions, shared dinner, a household dessert or fruit tradition, Turkish tea or coffee, conversation, and a warm ending.
4. Alcohol is not listed, filtered, priced, bundled, recommended, or promised.
5. Shared tables sell individual seats; private tables contain only the reserving party. Other guests are represented only with privacy-conscious, non-sensitive context.
6. Household diversity is real and welcome. No household is presented as representing every Turkish family. The positioning is: “This is how dinner happens in our home.”
7. The marketplace is host-created and platform-approved. Hosts shape their story, menu, dates, payout, and atmosphere; Sofra controls verification, capacity, safety, privacy, publication, payments, reviews, and intervention.

## Trust boundary

A host-created table is never publicly bookable until Sofra approves it. Exact home details remain private until the appropriate confirmed-booking stage. Public discovery is based on the table and its experience—not the appearance, gender, exact age, or sensitive traits of other travelers.
